# Enterprise SaaS & Venture Alignment

The strategic positioning of AfrEval as a lucrative, scalable enterprise SaaS model.

## The "tollbooth" mechanism

Every major commercial bank, telco, agricultural cooperative, and government agency intending to deploy an AI agent must route their models through the AfrEval API for continuous certification. This establishes **recurring subscription revenue**:

- Subscriptions grant access to continuous evaluation loops.
- Because frontier models are non-deterministic and update frequently, **a model that passes compliance in January may drift into non-compliance by March** — certification is a recurring need, not a one-time purchase ([Context Score calibration cadence](../subsystems/context-score-calibration.md)).

## Why the moat is defensible

- **Proprietary localized substrate** — WAXAL/AfroBench/afri-fertility-derived scoring no competitor trivially replicates.
- **Regulatory capture** — position as the default compliance layer as the AU enforces governance frameworks ([regulatory landscape](regulatory-landscape.md)).
- **Network effects** — open-source basic developer tools act as an acquisition channel; engineers integrating self-hosted eval suites transition to full enterprise SaaS (SOC 2 Type 2 compliance, air-gap deployments) as needs grow.

## Investor alignment

Aligned with the investment theses of African-focused funds:

- **Norrsken22** — leading African VC.
- **Google AI Futures Fund** — explicitly seeks AI infrastructure startups.

By capturing regulatory friction, AfrEval establishes "an unassailable data moat in the global AI landscape."
